# STP Camera Prototype V2

Test intent only; values are not final.

- Ball is the primary camera focus.
- Controlled player is a secondary framing constraint.
- Direction of play adds only a small rear offset.
- Normal ball weight: 68%.
- Counter-attack ball weight: 74%.
- Danger ball weight: 72%.
- Shot ball weight: 88%.
- Zoom and pivot transitions are intentionally slower and more progressive than V1.

Validation target: mobile landscape comfort and football-camera feel before any production lock.
